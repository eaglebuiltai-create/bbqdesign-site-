Evenings drop off more than they do in Roseville. Wind on the ridge lots is a real design input: a grill facing the wrong way is a grill you will not enjoy, and a light cover can be the difference between using the island in October and covering it. Oaks and the summer heat are both still here. You are not out of the Central Valley. You are just above the worst of the inversion.

Serrano, Blackstone, Promontory, Bass Lake, and Francisco Oaks do not share one rulebook. What they share is a review. A plan view and an elevation — the two drawings the kitchen designer produces, along with the 3D — are the packet a committee can actually react to. A mood board is not.

Access is the unglamorous part. A gate with a window for deliveries matters on pour day. Mention it. It is a scheduling fact, not a story about a particular job.

Questions

El Dorado Hills questions

El Dorado Hills is not a city. Who permits the work?

El Dorado County. There is no city building counter. The same things trigger a look as everywhere else we work: a gas line, a circuit, a sink, a cover. The permits guide is written as questions to take to that counter. It is general information, not a county determination.

What do Serrano, Blackstone, or Promontory usually want to see?

Those committees care about materials, height, and whether the structure reads as part of the house. Serrano in particular is known for a review before you build, not after. Bring them a plan and an elevation, not a verbal description. We cannot tell you what they will approve. We can hand you a drawing that is specific enough to ask.

The local photos include a fireplace. Can one be part of the kitchen?

The projects page shows an El Dorado Hills fireplace with a raised hearth beside a pool, separate from the kitchen photo. A fireplace can sit in the same yard as the island. New open masonry fireboxes are generally not what gets built; the site's own note is that a manufactured box clad in masonry is the usual path, and the air district has a say. The fireplace designer is the layout tool. The county is the code answer.
