The part that saves money

It tells you when something won't fit

Cutout deeper than the island

Several professional-series grills need more than 23 inches of depth. Design a 24 inch island around one and it will not go in. The tool flags it while you are still drawing.

No head-room under the grill

Drop a tall triple drawer under a grill and the two fight for the same space. The tool knows the head-room each unit leaves behind and what still fits beneath it.

The fridge sets the counter height

Six of the thirteen outdoor fridges we carry need more than 34.5 inches of clear opening underneath. You find out now rather than after the block is laid.

No landing space beside the grill

Somewhere to put the platter is the most common omission in a first design. The tool checks for it and says so.
