Questions

Common questions

How often does it go out?

Once a week, on Thursday. One project or one topic per issue. If there is nothing worth sending, we skip the week rather than pad it.

Is it free?

Yes, and there is nothing to buy. We build outdoor kitchens for a living, so the newsletter exists to be useful to people planning one — whether we end up building it or not.

Can I unsubscribe?

Any time. Every issue has a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. You do not have to reply or ask.

Do I have to live in Sacramento?

No. We build in Sacramento and the foothills, but the dimensions, cutouts, framing methods and permit guidance apply anywhere. The ZIP field is optional and only used to show pricing closer to your region.

Will you sell my email address?

No. We do not sell, rent or share subscriber lists. You will hear from EagleBuilt AI and no one else.
