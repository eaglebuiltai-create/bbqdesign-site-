The problem with leads

A name and a phone number is not a project

Most lead services capture contact details before the homeowner has thought about what they want. You pay for the privilege of finding out they were browsing, had no budget, or wanted something you do not build.

They have drawn it

A plan, a dimensioned front elevation and a 3D view of the island they actually want — not a description over the phone.

They have specified it

Real model numbers from ten manufacturers, each with its published cutout. You know exactly what you would be framing for.

They have seen a number

Construction and equipment priced separately and live as they built it. Sticker shock has already happened, or it has not.
