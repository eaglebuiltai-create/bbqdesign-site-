Privacy Policy

Plan & Elevation Studio. Effective October 2, 2026.

Plan & Elevation Studio is a drawing app for plans and elevations, published by EagleBuilt (John Simpson). It is built to work entirely on your device.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

  • Your projects: plans, elevations, names and client names you type in. They are saved as files in the app's own storage on your device and are removed when you delete a project or the app.
  • Your settings, including the company name and logo you choose for the title block.
  • Photos: the app only sees a photo when you pick one as your company logo. It keeps a small copy of that one image in its settings. It never reads your photo library otherwise.

Sharing

When you tap Share PDF or Share image, the app creates the file on your device and hands it to your device's share sheet. Where it goes from there (email, Messages, Files, a cloud drive) is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is a general drawing tool and is not directed at children.
