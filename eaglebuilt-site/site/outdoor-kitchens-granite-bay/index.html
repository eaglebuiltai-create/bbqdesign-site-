The yards around the lake

Granite Bay is not one subdivision. Los Lagos and Treelake are gated, with larger lots and a committee that will want to see a cover before it exists. Along Douglas Boulevard and Auburn-Folsom Road the lots change block to block: some open to the lake, some tucked into oaks, some already finished with a pool and a stamped patio that the island has to meet.

The shop being in 95746 does not change the ground. A lot that slopes toward the water, a setback off a property line, and an existing gas meter on the wrong side of the house all move the layout. Those are the things a drawing is for. The outdoor kitchen designer will tell you when a grill is deeper than the island or when a refrigerator sets the counter height. It will not see the slope. That still takes a visit.

Permits here are Placer County. Granite Bay has no city hall of its own. What usually triggers a permit is the utility or the roof, and the only authoritative answer is the county's, for your address.

Questions

Granite Bay questions

Who permits an outdoor kitchen in Granite Bay?

Granite Bay is unincorporated, so the counter is Placer County, not a city building department. What usually brings them in is a new gas line, an electrical circuit, a sink drain, or a roof over the island — not the block by itself. The permits guide is the longer version of that, and it is general. The county's answer for your address is the one that counts.

Do Los Lagos or Treelake get a say before anything is built?

A lot of Granite Bay communities have an architectural committee, and the gated ones are particular about height, color, and whether a cover can be seen from the street or the lake side. Ask them what they want to look at before the block is ordered. This is not a prediction of any committee's answer — it is the question to take them.

What does the Folsom Lake climate change about the island?

Summer afternoons are hot, the wind comes off the lake, and a winter night can frost. A cover, a counter that can sit in the sun, and which way the grill faces the breeze are the practical decisions. Oaks drop debris on anything left open. None of that is a price. It is why the drawing should be of this yard and not a catalog island.
