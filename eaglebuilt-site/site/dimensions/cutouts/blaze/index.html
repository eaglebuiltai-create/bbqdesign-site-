Depth check

Nine Blaze components need a 30-inch island

Anything needing more than about 23.5 inches of cutout depth leaves no workable structure in a 24-inch island. For Blaze that means the Professional LUX grills, both griddles, the pizza oven and everything refrigerated.

Professional LUX 34" & 44"

23.625" deep. The catalog note says it plainly: needs a 24 inch or deeper island — in practice, build 30.

LTE+ 30" and PRO 40" griddles

24.25" deep with a lift-assist hood. The 40" wants 26 inches of island by the manufacturer's own note.

Refrigeration and ice

24.25" to 24.5" deep across the fridges, beverage cooler and 50 lb ice maker. These also need a dedicated GFCI circuit.

Questions

Blaze cutout questions

What cutout does a 32-inch Blaze grill need?

30.625" wide × 8.5" tall × 21.25" deep for the BCM and Premium LTE+ four-burner. The LTE PRO 32" differs slightly at 30.75 × 9.5 × 21.25. Confirm against the manual that ships with the unit before cutting.

Can I put a triple drawer under a Blaze grill?

Not the tall ones. Blaze's own catalog notes mark the 16" and 30" triple drawers "tall — no grill above". A grill drops roughly 8.5 inches below the counter and the triple drawer wants 27.5, and a 36-inch counter has nowhere near that to give.

Do I need an island vent?

If you are running liquid propane, yes. Blaze lists the 6 × 14 inch island vent as required for LP — heavier-than-air gas needs a low path out of an enclosed island.

Can I mix Blaze with other brands?

Yes, and it is common. Each cutout is independent, so a Blaze grill over a different brand's combo is fine as long as both fit the island depth and the head-room works out. The designer checks that pairing for you.
