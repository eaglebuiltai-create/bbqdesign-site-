Questions

Blaze cutout questions

What cutout does a 32-inch Blaze grill need?

30 5/8" wide × 8 1/2" tall × 21 1/4" deep for the BCM and Premium LTE+ four-burner (BLZ-4BCM, BLZ-4LTE3). The LTE PRO 32" (BLZ-4LTEPRO) differs at 30 3/4" × 9 1/2" × 21 1/4". Always confirm with the manufacturer's spec sheet before cutting.

Can I put a triple drawer under a Blaze grill?

Not the tall ones. Blaze's catalog notes mark the 16" and 30" triple drawers "tall — no grill above". A grill drops roughly 8 1/2 inches below the counter and the triple drawer wants 27 1/2, and a 36-inch counter has nowhere near that to give.

Do I need an island vent?

If you are running liquid propane, yes. Blaze lists the 6 × 14 inch island vent (BLZ-ISLAND-VENT) as required for LP — heavier-than-air gas needs a low path out of an enclosed island.
