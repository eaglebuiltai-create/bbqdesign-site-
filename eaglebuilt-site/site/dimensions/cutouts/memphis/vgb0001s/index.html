Plan a 30-inch-deep island. This cutout is 24 1/2" deep; anything past about 23 1/2" leaves no workable structure in a 24-inch island.

  • Category: built-in pellet smoker
  • Fuel / power: Fuel: wood pellets; power: 110/120VAC dedicated circuit
  • Flange: none — fits inside the opening (Memphis product specifications: Dimensions 28" (width) vs Cut-out 30-1/4" (width). The grill body is narrower than the opening and sits inside it on built-in brackets; no trim flange.)
  • Overall size: 28" W × 28" H × 29" D
