Plan a 30-inch-deep island. This cutout is 28.1" deep; anything past about 23 1/2" leaves no workable structure in a 24-inch island.

  • Category: built-in grill
  • Fuel / power: Charcoal (kamado)
  • Flange: none — fits inside the opening (Kamado Joe Outdoor Kitchen Size Guide: C "Width of base of grill dome" 27.6 in; O "Width of circular counter cutout" 28.1 in. The ceramic body drops through the hole; no trim flange.)
  • Overall size: check spec sheet
  • Source: Round countertop hole, 28.1" diameter (W and D shown are the diameter); H is the height from the bottom of the grill feet to the top of the counter (20.8"). Add a latch notch at the front of the hole: 5.2" deep x 13.2" wide, 5.6" from the counter edge. Per the Kamado Joe Outdoor Kitchen Size Guide (also in the Built-In guide PDF). The grill sits on ceramic feet.
